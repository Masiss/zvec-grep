# R4 resource compliance & agent-plan discovery — Gate A/B/C

index change; frozen target 0.5722 unchanged. 25 workflow tests pass.

## Gate A — resource contract compliance: **PASS**

**Contract verification first.** `REPORT-CONTRACT.md` "Budget and stop
conditions": ≤3 rounds, ≤6 tool calls, ≤32 files, final pack ≤24 KiB,
cumulative tool content ≤64 KiB. The contract's tool-content bound governs
content *delivered to the agent*; internal candidate reads during ranking are
source reads, not tool content. The previous `tool_content_bytes`
instrumentation conflated the two (it counted 0.7–1.36 MB of internal rank
materialization), and that conflation — not an actual delivery violation — was
the audit's finding.

Fixes:
1. Accounting split: `WorkflowStats::tool_content_bytes` now measures
   *delivered* content only (final pack + gap texts, computed in `finish()`),
   and internal rank reads moved to `ranked_content_bytes` (reported, no
   contract bound). Regression test updated accordingly.
2. Real delivery violation found and fixed: gap details were unbounded — every
   rejected candidate appended a text line, up to **178 KB** delivered per
   case. Gap delivery is now coalesced (`MAX_GAP_DETAILS = 8` per code, full
   event count kept in `WorkflowStats::gap_events`), which is diagnostics
   compression, not evidence reduction.
3. The measurement arm now runs the **full contract budget** (3 rounds,
   6 tool calls, 32 files).

**Result under the full contract budget: recall unchanged — macro 0.3194,
pool 12/25, selected 6/25, pack ≤24 KiB, delivered ≤26.6 KiB per case
(<64 KiB), citation 1.000.** The previous 24-call/96-file budget bought nothing;
the workflow is compliant with no quality loss.

- Selection conversion remains the second wall: 14 pooled gold → 4 selected
  under the 24 KiB/10-item pack. A better plan cannot fix this alone.

## Why, and the one next step

The two binding walls, now both re-confirmed under the contract budget:
1. **Selection conversion** (pool gold → pack gold, currently ~50% and worse
   when the pool grows): the 24 KiB pack, the 10-item cap and region-blind
   allocation discard valid pooled evidence.
2. **Blind-plan identifier quality**: without corpus access a planner cannot
   name real identifiers; semantic routing of plan phrasing helps (pool
   12→14/25) but the pack cannot carry the extra evidence.

Next step (one proposal, not started): fix selection conversion under the
contract budget first — score-aware byte allocation across subjects with the
hybrid rank as the score — and only then re-run the *same* frozen E3 plan.
Success criterion: selected ≥10/25 with the 14/25 pool unchanged; if selection
still discards pooled gold at 24 KiB, the pack budget itself needs an owner
decision.
